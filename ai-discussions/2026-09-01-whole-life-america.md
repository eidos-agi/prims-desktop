Enough of Daniel exists that the shape of the system is clearly visible (work, companies, digital infrastructure, calendar/people seeds), but nowhere near enough that Daniel's life could be reconstructed from it. Concentrated artifact volume in ~8 domains must not fake completeness.

If a domain has no Prim, connector, representation, **or** explicit deferred stub, the Life Index is incomplete. The registry must know the negative space.

### Status key
- **live** — usable packs + honest continuous path in
- **seed** — real artifacts, not a usable model
- **stub** — need named; little or wrong-shaped data
- **missing** — not modeled (negative space made explicit here)
- **deferred** — explicit “we don’t model this yet”

### Depth reminder
`ovf` / “a vehicle Prim exists” earns almost nothing. Mature means which vehicle, ownership, VIN, insurance, registration, maintenance, recalls, accidents, expenses, drivers, documents, photos, condition, replacement plans, and links to people and places. Same bar for house, utility, person, etc.
